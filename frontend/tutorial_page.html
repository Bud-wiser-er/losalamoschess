Lesson 3: How Pieces Move

Learn how each piece moves in Los Alamos Chess

The Knight's L-Shaped Move

The Knight moves in an "L" shape: two squares in one direction and one square perpendicular, or one square in one direction and two squares perpendicular. It's the only piece that can jump over other pieces!

🔑 Key Points

  • Knights move in an L-shape pattern
  • They can jump over other pieces
  • Knights always land on a square of opposite color
  • From the center, a knight can reach 8 squares
  • Knights are especially powerful in closed positions
